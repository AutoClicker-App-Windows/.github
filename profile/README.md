## 🚀 What It Is
Auto Clicker for Windows is a friendly little tool that clicks your mouse for you. Whether you are running through a long software test, cycling through routine app actions, or just tired of tapping the same button over and over, it fires clicks at the interval you set. You stay in control of the speed, the position, and the button, while the tool handles the repetition. It stays out of the way and does one job well.

## ⭐ Key Features
> Everything you need to put repetitive clicking on autopilot, and nothing you don't.

- 🕒 **Custom timing** — Dial in the delay between clicks down to the millisecond for a fast or gentle pace.
- 📍 **Target any spot** — Lock clicks to a fixed screen position or follow the cursor wherever it goes.
- 🖱️ **Every click type** — Pick left, right, single, or double clicks to match what the task needs.
- ⌨️ **Instant hotkeys** — Start and stop clicking with a single keyboard shortcut, hands off the mouse.
- 🔁 **Loop or count** — Run a set number of clicks or keep going nonstop for long, unattended jobs.

## 🎯 First Steps
1. Launch Auto Clicker and enter how often you want it to click.
2. Choose the mouse button and whether you want single or double clicks.
3. Decide between clicking at the cursor or a pinned screen position.
4. Set a click limit, or leave it looping.
5. Tap your hotkey to begin, and tap it again to stop.
